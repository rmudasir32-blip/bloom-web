Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about BloomCycle, predictions, and privacy policies.

How does BloomCycle calculate predictions?

+

BloomCycle uses standard clinical timelines. Your fertile window is defined as the 5 days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself. As you log more days, the localized algorithm adjusts based on your cycle consistency and recorded body signals.

Is my health data uploaded to external servers?

+

No. Your health records, daily logs, notes, and cycle history are saved entirely in your iPhone's local sandbox storage. We do not use remote databases or server tracking for health metrics. Your privacy is absolute.

How do I switch the app to Pregnancy Mode?

+

Open the app, go to the More tab, select Pregnancy Settings, and toggle Pregnancy Mode. Input your estimated due date, and your dashboards, calendars, and loggers will automatically adapt to gestational pregnancy trackers.

How can I delete my data or reset the app?

+

You can clear all local records by navigating to the My Profile sheet under the More tab and clicking the destructive Delete Account button. This completely wipes all keychain tokens, local cycle files, and settings snapshots from your device.
